Help & Support ↗
Can I edit the captions after generation?

Yes. Correct text, adjust timing, choose fonts and colors, reposition captions, and apply animated styles before export.

Which languages can I use?

Lekha Captions supports 115+ languages, including English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia, and Arabic.

Which plan includes 4K and translation?

Creator and Pro include 1080p HD + 4K export and translation. Starter includes 1080p HD export. All paid plans include 115+ languages and 100+ caption styles.

Will my plan automatically renew?

No. Plans are one-time purchases and do not auto-renew. Review your currency, any applicable taxes, and the final total at checkout.
